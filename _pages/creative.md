## The Process

Creating props and costumes for film combines problem-solving skills from engineering with artistic expression. Each piece requires understanding the story context, working within production constraints, and ensuring durability for on-set use. Many of my pieces are built from accessible materials like cardboard, paper, foam, and silicone.
